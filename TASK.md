## EPIC ESC: Three-Party Escrow, Commitments and Reputation (2026-09-29) - @Scope tasks

Goal: one buyer payment is held before anyone commits; the farmer and the trucker each accept before a deadline; a failed trucker gives the buyer a choice; money is never stuck; broken commitments cost reputation. Design (APPROVED 2026-09-30, decisions in §14): `specs/features/three-party-escrow-commitments-reputation.md`; code survey `specs/reports/escrow-flow-survey-2026-09-29.md`. P0 is done except the ESC-13 and ESC-44 runs.

Product rules set by the user on 2026-09-29 (ask before changing them): money is held before the farmer and the trucker confirm, and the trucker must accept. If only the trucker fails, the buyer chooses another truck, self-pickup or cancel (the only case in which a buyer can cancel a paid order). A farmer or trucker who cancels or declines a paid order gets penalty points and a lower rating, and can be tagged "Low reputation".

Phases (spec §13): P0 money safety, P1 commitments and the transport choice, P2 reputation, P3 pickup and drop-off evidence and no-shows, P4 optional extras.
