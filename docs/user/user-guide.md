# User Guide

## Welcome to WeredaConnect

WeredaConnect is a hyperlocal civic engagement platform designed for Ethiopian communities. This guide will help you get started and make the most of the app.

## Audience
- End users (community members)
- Community leaders
- Local government officials

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Download the App

**Android (Recommended)**:
- Download from Google Play Store
- Search for "WeredaConnect"
- Minimum requirement: Android 8.0+
- Recommended: 2GB RAM or more

**iOS** (Coming Soon):
- Available on App Store
- Minimum requirement: iOS 13+

### 2. Create an Account

1. Open WeredaConnect
2. Tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your:
   - Phone number
   - Full name
   - Wereda (district)
   - Preferred language (Amharic, Afan Oromo, or English)
4. Verify your phone number with the SMS code
5. Create a password

### 3. Choose Your Language

WeredaConnect supports three languages:
- **አማርኛ** (Amharic)
- **Afaan Oromoo** (Afan Oromo)
- **English**

You can change the language anytime in **Settings > Language**.

## Key Features

### 📝 Report Issues

**Report problems in your community**:

1. Tap the **"+"** button
2. Select **"Report Issue"**
3. Choose a category:
   - Infrastructure (roads, water, electricity)
   - Sanitation (waste, drainage)
   - Safety (crime, accidents)
   - Health (clinics, disease outbreaks)
   - Education (schools, supplies)
   - Other
4. Add details:
   - Title (brief description)
   - Description (what happened, when, where)
   - Photos (up to 5)
   - Location (auto-detected or manual)
5. Tap **"Submit"**

**Offline Mode**: You can create reports even without internet! They'll sync automatically when you're back online.

### 👀 View Reports

**See what others are reporting**:

1. Tap **"Reports"** tab
2. Browse reports by:
   - **Recent**: Latest reports first
   - **Nearby**: Closest to your location
   - **Category**: Filter by issue type
   - **Status**: Open, In Progress, Resolved
3. Tap a report to see details
4. Upvote reports you care about
5. Add comments to share information

### 💬 Community Discussions

**Engage with your community**:

1. Tap **"Discussions"** tab
2. Browse topics or create new ones
3. Share ideas, ask questions, organize events
4. Reply to others' posts
5. Follow topics you're interested in

### 📊 Track Progress

**Monitor issue resolution**:

1. Go to **"My Reports"**
2. See status updates:
   - **Pending**: Waiting for review
   - **Acknowledged**: Seen by officials
   - **In Progress**: Being worked on
   - **Resolved**: Issue fixed
   - **Closed**: No action needed
3. Receive notifications when status changes

### 🔔 Notifications

**Stay informed**:

- Report status updates
- Comments on your reports
- Replies to your discussions
- Community announcements
- Emergency alerts

Manage notifications in **Settings > Notifications**.

## Using WeredaConnect Offline

### Why Offline-First?

We know internet connectivity can be unreliable in many areas. WeredaConnect works **offline-first**, meaning:

✅ **You can use most features without internet**  
✅ **Your data is saved locally on your phone**  
✅ **Everything syncs automatically when you're online**

### What Works Offline?

- ✅ Create reports
- ✅ View previously loaded reports
- ✅ Add comments (will sync later)
- ✅ Browse discussions you've seen before
- ✅ View your profile and settings

### What Needs Internet?

- ❌ Load new reports from others
- ❌ Upload photos (queued until online)
- ❌ Receive real-time notifications
- ❌ See latest status updates

### Sync Indicator

Look for the sync icon in the top-right corner:
- **🟢 Green**: Connected, synced
- **🟡 Yellow**: Syncing...
- **🔴 Red**: Offline (will sync when online)
- **⏳ Pending**: Items waiting to sync

## Tips for Best Experience

### 📱 Save Data

WeredaConnect is designed to use minimal data:

1. **Download reports when on WiFi**: Reports are cached for offline viewing
2. **Compress photos**: Enable in Settings > Data Saver
3. **Sync only on WiFi**: Settings > Sync > WiFi Only
4. **Limit photo uploads**: Use 1-2 photos instead of 5

### 🔋 Save Battery

1. **Disable location tracking**: Settings > Location > Only When Using App
2. **Reduce sync frequency**: Settings > Sync > Manual Sync
3. **Dark mode**: Settings > Appearance > Dark Mode

### 🌍 Improve Location Accuracy

1. Enable GPS in phone settings
2. Allow WeredaConnect to access location
3. Wait a few seconds for GPS to lock
4. Manually adjust pin on map if needed

## Privacy & Safety

### Your Data

- **Private by default**: Only you see your personal info
- **Public reports**: Reports are visible to your community
- **Anonymous option**: Report without showing your name
- **Data control**: Delete your account anytime

### Reporting Safely

- **Don't share personal info**: Avoid phone numbers, addresses in reports
- **Be respectful**: No hate speech, harassment, or false information
- **Report abuse**: Flag inappropriate content
- **Emergency**: For urgent safety issues, call local authorities first
