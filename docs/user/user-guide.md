# User Guide

## Welcome to WeredaConnect

WeredaConnect is an offline-first agritech logistics platform designed for Ethiopian rural communities. It connects farmers, transport providers (truckers), and urban wholesalers/buyers through a marketplace that works even without reliable internet. This guide will help you get started and make the most of the app.

## Audience
- Farmers (crop sellers)
- Transport providers (truckers)
- Urban wholesalers and buyers

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Download the App

**Android (Recommended)**:
- Download from Google Play Store
- Search for "WeredaConnect"
- Minimum requirement: Android 8.0+
- Recommended: 2GB RAM or more

**iOS** (Coming Soon):
- Available on App Store
- Minimum requirement: iOS 13+

### 2. Create an Account

1. Open WeredaConnect
2. Tap **"Sign Up"**
3. Choose your role:
   - **Farmer** (sell crops, request transport)
   - **Transporter** (offer trucking services)
   - **Buyer** (browse and purchase crops)
4. Enter your:
   - Phone number
   - Full name
   - Wereda (district)
   - Preferred language (Amharic, Afan Oromo, or English)
5. Verify your identity with **Fayda ID** (Ethiopia's national digital ID)
6. Verify your phone number with the SMS code
7. Link your **Telebirr** account for payments

### 3. Choose Your Language

WeredaConnect supports three languages:
- **አማርኛ** (Amharic)
- **Afaan Oromoo** (Afan Oromo)
- **English**

You can change the language anytime in **Settings > Language**.

## Key Features

### 🌾 Create Crop Listings

**List your harvest for sale using voice or text**:

1. Tap the **"+"** button
2. Select **"New Crop Listing"**
3. Add crop details:
   - Crop type (teff, wheat, maize, coffee, sesame, etc.)
   - Quantity (quintals/kg)
   - Price per quintal
   - Quality grade
   - Harvest date
   - Photos (up to 5)
   - Pickup location (auto-detected or manual)
4. Use **voice-to-text** to describe your listing in Amharic, Afan Oromo, or English — ideal for low-literacy users
5. Tap **"Publish Listing"**

**Offline Mode**: You can create crop listings even without internet! They'll sync automatically when you're back online.

### 🚛 Find Transport

**Connect with truckers to move your goods**:

1. Tap **"Transport"** tab
2. Select **"Request Transport"**
3. Enter details:
   - Pickup location (your farm or local collection point)
   - Destination (market, warehouse, buyer address)
   - Cargo type and weight
   - Preferred pickup date
4. Browse available transporters by:
   - **Nearby**: Closest to your pickup location
   - **Price**: Lowest fare first
   - **Rating**: Highest-rated transporters
5. Send a transport request
6. Agree on terms and confirm booking

### 🛒 Browse Marketplace

**Find crops to buy or transport jobs to take**:

1. Tap **"Marketplace"** tab
2. Browse listings by:
   - **Recent**: Latest crop listings first
   - **Nearby**: Closest to your location
   - **Crop Type**: Filter by teff, wheat, coffee, etc.
   - **Price Range**: Set your budget
3. Tap a listing to see full details
4. Contact the seller directly
5. Place an order and proceed to escrow payment

### 💰 Escrow Payments (Telebirr)

**Secure transactions with Telebirr escrow**:

1. Buyer places an order and pays into **escrow** via Telebirr
2. Funds are held securely — neither buyer nor seller can access them yet
3. Seller prepares the goods for transport
4. Transporter picks up and delivers the cargo
5. Buyer confirms delivery using a **QR delivery token**
6. Funds are released to the seller and transporter

**Important**: Never send money outside of escrow. The escrow system protects both buyers and sellers from fraud.

### 📍 Track Deliveries

**Monitor your shipments from farm to market**:

1. Go to **"My Deliveries"**
2. See delivery status:
   - **Confirmed**: Order accepted, awaiting pickup
   - **Picked Up**: Transporter has the cargo
   - **In Transit**: On the way to destination
   - **Delivered**: Arrived — awaiting buyer confirmation
   - **Completed**: Buyer confirmed, payment released
3. View GPS location and photo evidence from the transporter
4. Scan or present your **QR delivery token** to confirm receipt
5. Rate the transporter and seller after delivery

### 🔔 Notifications

**Stay informed about your trades and deliveries**:

- New orders on your crop listings
- Transport request updates
- Delivery status changes
- Escrow payment confirmations
- Price alerts for crops you follow
- Marketplace matches for your saved searches

Manage notifications in **Settings > Notifications**.

## Using WeredaConnect Offline

### Why Offline-First?

We know internet connectivity can be unreliable in many rural areas. WeredaConnect works **offline-first**, meaning:

✅ **You can use most features without internet**
✅ **Your data is saved locally on your phone**
✅ **Everything syncs automatically when you're online**

### What Works Offline?

- ✅ Create crop listings (voice-to-text included)
- ✅ View previously loaded marketplace listings
- ✅ Browse saved transporter profiles
- ✅ Review your orders and delivery history
- ✅ Draft transport requests (will sync later)
- ✅ View your profile, wallet summary, and settings

### What Needs Internet?

- ❌ Load new marketplace listings from others
- ❌ Upload photos (queued until online)
- ❌ Process escrow payments via Telebirr
- ❌ Receive real-time delivery tracking updates
- ❌ Send and receive messages

### Sync Indicator

Look for the sync icon in the top-right corner:
- **🟢 Green**: Connected, synced
- **🟡 Yellow**: Syncing...
- **🔴 Red**: Offline (will sync when online)
- **⏳ Pending**: Items waiting to sync (crop listings, orders, messages)

## Tips for Best Experience

### 📱 Save Data

WeredaConnect is designed to use minimal data:

1. **Download listings when on WiFi**: Marketplace listings are cached for offline browsing
2. **Compress photos**: Enable in Settings > Data Saver
3. **Sync only on WiFi**: Settings > Sync > WiFi Only
4. **Limit listing photos**: Use 1-2 photos instead of 5

### 🔋 Save Battery

1. **Disable background location**: Settings > Location > Only When Using App
2. **Reduce sync frequency**: Settings > Sync > Manual Sync
3. **Dark mode**: Settings > Appearance > Dark Mode

### 🌍 Improve Location Accuracy

1. Enable GPS in phone settings
2. Allow WeredaConnect to access location
3. Wait a few seconds for GPS to lock
4. Manually adjust pin on map if needed
5. Accurate location helps buyers and transporters find you faster

## Privacy & Safety

### Your Data

- **Private by default**: Your financial details and personal info are only visible to you
- **Fayda ID verified**: All users are identity-verified to prevent fraud
- **Escrow protected**: Funds are held securely until delivery is confirmed
- **Data control**: Delete your account and data anytime

### Financial Safety

- **Use escrow for all trades**: Never send money directly to a seller or transporter outside the app
- **Verify delivery with QR tokens**: Always scan or present the QR code to confirm receipt
- **Check ratings and reviews**: Trade with verified, well-rated users
- **Report suspicious activity**: Flag accounts or listings that seem fraudulent

### Marketplace Safety

- **Be accurate**: Describe your crops honestly — quality, quantity, and condition
- **Be responsive**: Reply to inquiries and update listing status promptly
- **Report abuse**: Flag inappropriate listings or users
- **Emergency**: For urgent safety issues, call local authorities first
